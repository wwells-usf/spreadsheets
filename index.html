BUS 125 · Excel Companion

Simple support for learning Excel and data visualization.

This site contains supplemental learning materials for an in-person course focused on Microsoft Excel and data visualization.

The examples, visualizations, and interactive activities are designed to reinforce concepts introduced during class and provide another way to explore how spreadsheets work. This is not intended to be a comprehensive Excel reference, nor is it a replacement for Microsoft’s official documentation, course materials, or other established learning resources.

Think of this as a growing collection of simple explanations, visual examples, and interactive demonstrations you can revisit when a concept needs another look.
