Define the concept first

Conditional logic means Excel can look, test, and respond.

1

Look

Excel looks at a value, text entry, formula result, or cell status.

2

Test

Excel checks a rule or condition such as “Is this below 1000?”

3

Respond

Excel does something based on the result: format it, return a label, count it, or include it in a total.

Definition: Conditional logic means Excel checks a condition and then does something based on whether that condition is true or false.
Look at value: $850 Test: Is it below $1,000? Respond: Highlight it
